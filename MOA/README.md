# MOA Antarctic Dataset Documentation

## Overview

This dataset contains two important Antarctic datasets from NASA's Making Earth System Data Records for Use in Research Environments (MEaSUREs) Program: MOA2004 and MOA2014. These datasets provide snow grain size maps and surface morphology maps of Antarctica, serving as crucial data resources for studying Antarctic climate change and glacier dynamics.

## Dataset Details

### MOA2014 Dataset

**Overview**

The MOA2014 dataset includes two image maps - a snow grain size map and a surface morphology map - provided at two different resolutions of 125 m and 750 m. The maps were produced from composited image swath data acquired by the Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer (MODIS) over Antarctica for the 2013-2014 austral summer season.

**Technical Parameters**
- **Parameters**: Snow Grain Size, Surface Morphology
- **Platform**: Aqua, Terra
- **Sensor**: MODIS
- **Data Formats**: IMG, Binary, GeoTIFF, ASCII, ENVI, Shapefile, GMT, KML
- **Temporal Coverage**: 
  - 27 October 2013 to 16 December 2013
  - 6 November 2013 to 8 March 2014
- **Temporal Resolution**: Not applicable
- **Spatial Resolution**: 125 m, 750 m
- **Spatial Reference System**: WGS 84 / Antarctic Polar Stereographic (EPSG:3031)
- **Spatial Coverage**: N:-60° S:-90° E:180° W:-180°

### MOA2004 Dataset

**Overview**

The MOA2004 dataset also includes snow grain size maps and surface morphology maps provided at 125 m and 750 m resolutions. These maps were produced from composited image swath data acquired by MODIS over Antarctica for the 2003-2004 austral summer season.

**Technical Parameters**
- **Parameters**: Snow Grain Size, Surface Morphology
- **Platform**: Aqua, Terra
- **Sensor**: MODIS
- **Data Formats**: IMG, Binary, GeoTIFF, ASCII, ENVI, Shapefile, GMT, KML
- **Temporal Coverage**: 
  - 20 November 2003 to 29 February 2004
  - 1 November 2003 to 17 December 2003
- **Temporal Resolution**: Not applicable
- **Spatial Resolution**: 125 m, 750 m
- **Spatial Reference System**: WGS 84 / Antarctic Polar Stereographic (EPSG:3031)
- **Spatial Coverage**: N:-60° S:-90° E:180° W:-180°
